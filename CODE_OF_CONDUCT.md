# Community code of conduct

TGent is an engineering community. Participate with respect, precision, and consideration for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Expected behavior includes giving actionable feedback, discussing ideas rather than attacking people, respecting privacy, crediting others' work, and accepting corrections. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports about conduct should use a private maintainer contact channel; do not publish personal or sensitive details in an issue. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and a pattern of behavior.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
