# Commercial offerings and licensing

TGent is open-source software under `AGPL-3.0-only`. The license permits commercial use; it also carries source-sharing obligations, including for modified versions offered to users over a network.

Official commercial offerings may include:

- hosted account, discovery, synchronization, and relay services;
- signed and managed release channels;
- managed deployment and upgrades;
- enterprise authentication, policy, audit, and infrastructure integration;
- prioritized engineering support and service-level agreements.

These offerings can be paid even though the source is open. Purchasing a service does not remove or reduce rights already granted under the AGPL.

Alternative commercial license terms may be available for components for which the maintainers hold sufficient rights, for organizations that cannot meet AGPL obligations. Availability is evaluated case by case and is not implied by this document. Start a GitHub Discussion marked as a commercial inquiry; do not include confidential information in the initial message.

This document is a product policy summary, not a substitute for the license text or legal advice.
